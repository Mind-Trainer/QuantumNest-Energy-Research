# Related work and novelty boundary

This focused review used publisher pages, author copies and Crossref metadata. The closest overlap is classical uncertainty-aware HEMS, quantum EV scheduling and constraint-preserving variational circuits. The recurring techniques in QuantumNest are known. The proposed research contribution is the reproducible comparison of exact, domain-pruned one-hot encodings on a physically checked appliance-plus-EV problem, with explicit negative and positive findings. A claim of algorithmic first use is unsupported.

## Next experiment for a paper

Use preregistered comparisons on more than two independently variable devices and a measured distribution of typical overlaps. Check exactness on that expanded suite, separate model compilation from variational training and downstream sampling, and compare against direct feasible sampling and an optimized classical formulation. A stronger study would evaluate multiple transpiler connectivity graphs, optimizer restarts, independently varied parameter seeds, and held-out PV outcomes. The current ideal and synthetic-noise comparisons support a bounded artifact report and a conference submission draft centered on methodology and limitations.
